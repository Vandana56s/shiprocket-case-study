# Strategic Recommendation: Marketing Spend Optimization

**To:** CMO  
**From:** Growth & Analytics  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Subject:** Q3 Budget Reallocation & 20% Paid Spend Cut

### Recommendation

To cut paid marketing spend by 20% (~₹3.2 Lakhs of ₹16 Lakhs total spend) without hurting revenue:

1. **Completely eliminate Influencer spend** (saving ~₹3.0 Lakhs). Influencer is deeply unprofitable with an **LTV:CAC of 0.6x** (CAC ₹2,100 vs. LTV ₹1,250), losing ₹850 on contribution margin per customer acquired. Furthermore, its CAC worsened by +38% between January and June.
2. **Trim Meta Ads budget by 5%** (saving remaining ~₹20,000–₹30,000) by pausing creative ad sets with declining conversion rates.

### Channels to Protect & Scale

- **Google Ads:** Protect and scale. Google delivers the strongest portfolio performance with a **2.4x LTV:CAC**, a **31.2% repeat purchase rate**, and stable monthly CAC.
- **Affiliate:** Maintain current spend. Operating at an **1.8x LTV:CAC** on a pay-per-conversion model, it provides steady volume with zero spend volatility.

### Key Risks & Guardrails

- **Top-of-Funnel Risk:** Pausing Influencer campaigns could dampen indirect organic and direct traffic.
- **Monitoring:** Track weekly Organic signups and branded Google Search volume. If Organic new customer acquisition drops by >8% over 3 consecutive weeks, redeploy ₹40,000 into performance micro-influencers contracted exclusively via trackable discount codes rather than upfront sponsorship fees.
